## The fine print coupon sites don't mention

This is where the aggregator pages go quiet, and it matters more than choosing between two 10% codes.

**Refunds depend on which plan you buy.** GreenCloud offers a 7-day money-back window, but only for **new customers on their first VPS** — and the policy explicitly excludes specially discounted plans. That's consistent with what the cart itself says: every Budget KVM Sale plan is labeled "No refund/Money back on this plan," including the Singapore location. So the $15 Sale 1 order is effectively final; a standard SSDKVM or Ryzen plan as a first purchase gives you a week to change your mind.

**Renewal pricing is not confirmed for most plans.** The price tracker lists renewal rates as unconfirmed across the lineup, meaning the price you renew at may differ from your intro rate. Monthly billing keeps this low-risk; with a 12- or 36-month prepay you've locked the term anyway, but check the renewal figure shown in your cart before committing.

**APAC traffic is roughly half of US/EU.** The pattern holds across every KVM line, and Hong Kong is the strictest of all: 500 GB on the smallest tiers, China-direction bandwidth capped at 10Mbps. If your users are in Asia, run the numbers against a Japan or Singapore node before assuming the US price/perf transfers.

**Stock moves in batches.** On the store page at the time of checking, popular locations including New York, Ashburn, Los Angeles, Dallas, Amsterdam, and Ogden showed zero units available, while Chicago DC2, Jacksonville, and Kansas City had stock. Hardware also varies by location — some cities run NVMe on EPYC Rome/Milan, others SSD RAID-10 on older E5v4 chips. Waiting rarely improves your options when a good location shows available.
